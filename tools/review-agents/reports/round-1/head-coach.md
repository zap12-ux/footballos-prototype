## Learnings

**What worked**

The Westmoor dashboard opened Defense v1 in review in one action. The invite survived a return with its role and unit intact, and an explicitly saved objective survived approval.

**What did not**

During the Sunday check I had to infer who owed a third-down rewrite, and I could not establish parent access. During editing, changing units before Save silently removed the added protection objective.

**What to try next**

Give a fresh Head Coach one unfinished Westmoor decision, an unsaved objective and a parent access question. Count how many decisions have a recorded owner and disposition by the end of the Sunday check, and verify that the exact objective survives the detour.
