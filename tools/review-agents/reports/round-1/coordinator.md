## Interview answers

**1. First impression in one sentence.**

I see one staff room where I can connect the Sunday cut to the play call the assistants teach on Monday.

**2. Did the product match what the landing page led you to expect? Where not?**

The landing page promised reviewed numbers, evidence and approved versions. I did get 31/44 and an approval stamped with my name. I expected the film at 10:40, and I expected v3 to be there after refresh. Neither held up.

**3. Walk me through your normal week. Where would this fit, and where does it fight your routine?**

I tag our own film Saturday in Hudl, work the opponent’s fronts and pressures into a spreadsheet Sunday, then put the install in front of the line coach Monday. I can use the review-basis switch at that Sunday step. Retyping both film times and losing the approved version puts my spreadsheet straight back on the desk.

**4. What confused you? What did you have to figure out?**

“Their defense” made sense while scouting. When I opened Plans, it gave me our defense, and I had to find my offense again. Add also put a note on the list before Save draft had protected it.

**5. Where did you trust the AI's output, where did you not, and what made the difference?**

I trust an abstention I can explain: Play 6 Down identified the missing marker, and reopening the history showed my reason. I used 31/44 as a question worth preparing. I would not tell the line that Westmoor always showed four down from a different six-second video.

**6. What would you stop using it over?**

I would stop if my current approved version disappeared between Sunday and Monday. I saw my own v3 and the line coach’s question before the reload; afterwards I could choose only the old approved v1 or the draft v2.

**7. What would you change first? Second?**

First keep the saved and approved work there when I come back, and protect edits when I leave the draft to look at film. Second take me from each tendency and Monday cue to the actual original snap.

**8. What should they not touch?**

Keep the numerator, denominator and review-basis badge on each finding, the separately listed small samples, and the way a label correction keeps my reason. I also want the version number and approver to stay visible.

**9. Would you use this during the season? Would you recommend it to your staff? Answer yes, no or conditional, with the condition.**

No, I would not use this version as my in-season plan. No, I would not recommend that my staff rely on it yet. I would reconsider after our approved version survives a fresh open and my assistants can reach the cited plays.

**10. Anything else.**

Our own Scout 02 note made it into Monday’s version with a clear teaching point. I liked being able to say the film was inconclusive and still tell the center and left guard exactly what to rehearse.

**11. Is the problem this solves one you would switch tools for? What do you use today and what would make this better enough to switch?**

Yes, connecting reviewed film to the install is a problem I would switch for. Today I use Hudl for cuts, a spreadsheet for fronts and pressures, and a shared call sheet. I would switch when the OL coach can reopen my approved cue and get both source snaps without me sending the cuts separately.

**12. Would you choose to use this on your own time, unprompted? If not, why not?**

No. On my own Sunday night I would open Hudl and my sheet first. I just watched a reload take us back from my approved v3 to the sample draft, and I would have to do the work twice.

**13. What single outcome would you measure to know it worked for you this season?**

I would track the share of approved weekly install cues whose exact supporting snaps the line coach can open from the saved plan without asking me to resend a clip.

The 31/44 reviewed finding and the explicit Unknown reason let the staff write a precise center/left-guard drill without asserting an unverified call. The named cue and question survived the in-app approval round trip.

**What did not**

The 10:40 evidence path played a short substitute, the install offered plain-text references, and refreshing the approved mock v3 returned the game to v1/v2 without the two entries.

**What to try next**

Run the same Westmoor Sunday-to-Monday handoff with a line coach after a fresh browser open. Count approved cues for which the coach can reproduce both original snaps without a separately sent cut number.
