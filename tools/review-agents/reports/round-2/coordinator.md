## Learnings

**What worked**

An unadded Gold protection entry survived Personnel, and a saved cue and three open comments were preserved through offensive v2 approval and reload.

**What did not**

The OC could approve v6 with a typed Slate protection cue that then became inaccessible. Neither of the two W1 film references supplied the actual snap.

**What to try next**

Over four game weeks, ask the line coach to open each protection cue’s original snap from the approved version in at most two actions, then record the fraction that succeeds.
