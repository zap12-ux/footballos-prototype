The first page promises recorded approvals and private staff work. I saw the unit and expiry on my invitation and the signer on the current plan. I lost that signer on the superseded version, and I could not establish the audience of a plan share link.

**3. Walk me through your normal week. Where would this fit, and where does it fight your routine?**

Saturday I cut film in Hudl. Sunday I get seven assistants lined up and settle the install. The Westmoor game card and pending defense approval would save a round of texts. Two offensive drafts labeled draft would put us right back in the shared-sheet argument about which copy is current.

**4. What confused you? What did you have to figure out?**

The dashboard told me sixteen cuts needed review, then put me in Opponent Scouting. I had to go back through Our Team Film. I also had to compare three upload dates because the first ready file was four days older than the latest upload.

**5. Where did you trust the AI's output, where did you not, and what made the difference?**

I trusted the scouting page enough to follow the 19-of-54 trips figure to the film and look at what was left out. I would ask the defensive coordinator to check the nine-of-eleven run figure play by play. It included unreviewed labels, and I could still approve it as written.

**6. What would you stop using it over?**

I would stop putting team film in here if I could upload the wrong clip and have no way to remove it. I watched my test clip reach Ready and still could not take it down.

**7. What would you change first? Second?**

First, give me a delete action on the film itself with a confirmation and a record I can find afterward. Second, let me see the signer, time and override on every approved version, even after we replace it.

**8. What should they not touch?**

Keep the week and seven open tasks together. Keep the assistant’s role, unit and expiry visible after I invite them. Keep the numerator, denominator and excluded snaps directly on the scouting finding.

**9. Would you use this during the season? Would you recommend it to your staff? Answer yes, no or conditional, with the condition.**

Conditional for my own in-season use with synthetic film and a coordinator cross-checking the weekly plan. No for recommending it across the staff with player film until I can delete an asset, verify a shared link’s audience and recover earlier approvals.

**10. Anything else.**

When I approve a plan, tell me which findings still use unreviewed labels and take me to the plays. The comment on the seeded defense plan explicitly asked for a rewording, and one click let me sign with that comment still open.
