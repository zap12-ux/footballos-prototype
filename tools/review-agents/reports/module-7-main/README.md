## A revision can be lost on the way back to practice focus

On `/#/demo`, all six plays had saved decisions and the practice focus had been edited. Reopening Play 5 and choosing **Change to Grade 3** displayed a check on the new grade, the existing **Saved · Grade 2** badge, and a message asking for a reason to save the revision. **Continue to practice focus** remained enabled. Selecting it returned directly to the focus with Play 5 still counted at Grade 2. Reopening Play 5 again had cleared the pending reason choice. A coach who leaves at this point has to reenter the change. A pending-state label and a choice to finish or discard the revision before leaving would make the consequence visible.
